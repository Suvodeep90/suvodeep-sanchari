Before you ask

Questions

What’s the weather like in December?

Crisp days and cold nights. Over the last ten years, those four days at the lodge averaged highs around 52 °F (11 °C) and lows just above freezing, with rain on about one day in three and the occasional dusting of snow. Each day in the itinerary shows its own outlook — it switches to a live forecast about two weeks before. Bring layers, a warm coat, and shoes you can walk hills in.

Can I bring a plus-one or my children?

What should I wear?

There is no dress code — not for the ceremony, not for anything else. Wear what you feel good in. The only thing we’d push is a warm coat and shoes you can walk hills in; December in the mountains is genuinely cold.

Everything — ceremony, dinner and brunch — happens at Blue Cedar Lodge, so there is no venue-hopping once you arrive. You will want a car to reach Gatlinburg and to explore the park. Confirm add parking details or any airport shuttle.
